How well does coherence in various cortical networks predict lexico-semantic convergence?


Here we focus on a subset of distinct networks, consisting of multiple interacting cortical regions: the Default Mode Network (DMN), the Frontal Parietal Network (FPN), the Ventral Attention Network (VAT), and the Dorsal Attention Network (DAT).

Using the LME model described in the background section, we report on our results for each network in two conditions: the heterogeneous gender condition, and the homogeneous gender condition.

The results here are calculated using a multivariate Wald-test to ascertain the degree to which the additive parameter estimates and their variance contribute to a coherent difference across all fNIRS channels belonging to the network. Significant results indicate that the effects reported are unexpected given the null hypothesis (that there is no coherent affect when adding together the parameter estimates and accounting for their associated variance) at a given alpha level.

For all figures, ◆ = p < .05; ⟐ = p < .01
